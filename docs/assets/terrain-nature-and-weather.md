# Frostmark assets: terrain, nature and weather

Research only. Nothing was downloaded. Every licence below was read on the source's own page on 2026-10-16 unless the row says `unverified`. "Checked" is the date the licence was read.

## Best picks for Frostmark

For the low-poly, flat-shaded look, start with **Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack** (CC0, 150 models, and the Poly Pizza listing shows snow-covered pine, dead tree, birch, bush and rock variants by the same author) plus **KayKit Forest Nature Pack** (CC0, 100+ free models on one 1024px gradient atlas, which is light for the web build). Add **flo-bit's Low Poly Nature Pack** (CC0) for ready-made winter pines. For ground, sky and rock, use **Poly Haven** (snow textures, winter/night HDRIs, a pine and fir tree) and **ambientCG** (Snow/Ice/Rock PBR). Both are CC0 and need no credit. For terrain and weather tooling, **Terrain3D** (MIT, Compatibility/WebGL support but web is marked experimental) and **Sky3D** (MIT, Compatibility renderer supported) are the strong candidates. HTerrain is a fallback. Realistic PBR textures and HDRIs will clash with flat shading, so treat them as colour sources: downsample them, or use only the albedo.

Two things to keep in mind. Anything marked CC-BY needs a credits file. No NC or ND assets were found among the entries listed. Several free packs on itch.io state no licence at all, and those are marked `unverified` and should not be used until the author confirms terms.

## Gaps and caveats

- **Licence-unclear packs.** The Tryall and Nowhere0 winter packs fit best but state no licence on their pages. Ask the authors in writing before use, or skip them.
- **Poly Pizza.** Its search and model pages did not show licences, and model pages were erroring during research. Open each model's page and record the licence before use. Do not assume the Quaternius snow models are CC0 just because his own packs are.
- **Realism mismatch.** Poly Haven models and PBR textures from Poly Haven and ambientCG are CC0 but realistic and heavy. Use them for colour, normal detail and lighting, not for the main look.
- **Web export.** Terrain3D lists web as experimental. HTerrain's master targets 4.6+. Test a web build of any terrain tool in the first slice. Weather System was listed for 4.2 only. Spatial Ice Shader needs porting to 4.x syntax.
- **Missing content.** No free aurora skybox or HDRI was found, and no ore-deposit or ice-formation pack under CC0. Ore nodes can be made by recolouring rocks (Cosmo, KayKit, Quaternius). The Sketchfab search found only CC BY ice models; Sketchfab CC0 filtering was not completed.
- **Not covered.** Heightmap data was not found as a standalone asset. Texture formats and resolutions for Poly Haven and ambientCG were not itemised on the pages read. The Godot Asset Library site itself was not reachable, so addons were checked through godotengine.org asset pages and search listings.
- **No NC or ND assets** were found in this category. CC-BY entries (Kyrise, Humus skyboxes, Sketchfab ice cluster) need a credits file. The Kyrise pack is free but its listed attribution line is mandatory.
- **Paid tiers.** KayKit Extra ($9.99+) and Dragonforge ice tiers are paid. Their free tiers are listed separately where it matters.
