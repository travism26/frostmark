# Free audio (SFX and music) for Frostmark

Research only. No files were downloaded. All licences were read from the source's own page on 2026-10-16 unless the Licence cell says otherwise. Where a primary page was blocked (403, 404, or a loading screen), the row says so and the licence is marked `unverified`.

## Best picks for Frostmark

1. **Kenney Impact Sounds** (CC0, 130 files) and **Kenney Interface Sounds** (CC0, 100 files) cover the core SFX: chops, metal and wood impacts, and clicks. Both are CC0 with no credit needed, and Kenney's own page says so.
2. **OpenGameArt "Medieval sound effects - Weapon impacts"** (CC0, WAV, 48 described sounds) covers axe, sword and blade hits for melee and combat. The download is about 156 MB, so trim it before shipping.
3. **OpenGameArt "Snow theme" by Cleyton Kauffman** (CC0, WAV/OGG/FLAC) is the best cold ambient music pick so far. Credit is optional. For a looping tavern/outpost or tense exploration track, the cynicmusic tracks are CC0 too but limited in format and variety. Incompetech (CC BY) has the largest music library, but it requires credit.

Caution for a public web build: several licences forbid selling or distributing raw files on a standalone basis (Pixabay, ZapSplat, Mixkit, Sonniss). Embedding the sounds in the game is generally fine, but check each licence before shipping a folder of raw audio. Avoid ccMixter and anything NC for a build that could be monetised.

## Gaps and caveats

- **Wolves:** no CC0 wolf howl or growl was found. Look at Freesound with the CC0 filter, or record a commission. Pay attention to the NC clause.
- **Pickaxe and stone chops:** no dedicated CC0 pack was found. Kenney Impact Sounds is the closest candidate.
- **Blizzard and wind ambience:** no CC0 pack was found. Freesound with the CC0 filter is the best next step.
- **Blocked or unverified primary pages:** Sonniss (loading screen), ZapSplat (403), Musopen (403), ccMixter track licences (only the site footer checked), Mixkit game use (not on primary page), Incompetech exact credit wording and version. These are marked `unverified` or secondary.
- **FreePD:** the site says it is closed and hosting has ceased, and no licence text was found. Not recommended.
- **Musopen:** primary terms returned 403. Not checked; treat as `unverified`.
- **itch.io free audio:** only one itch.io pack checked (kirrifant). A broader itch.io search was not done.
- **Kenney formats:** the Kenney asset pages do not state file formats. The Calinou repo confirms the Interface Sounds originals were OGG and the port is WAV. Other Kenney packs are unverified.
- **Credit lines:** check each track on the source page before shipping. For cc-by items, put the credit in the game's credits screen.
- **Licence checks** were done by reading the source page's text. This is not legal advice; re-check on download, since licence text can change.
