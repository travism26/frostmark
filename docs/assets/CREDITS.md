## Optional courtesy credits (CC0, not required)

Credit is not required for Kenney, Quaternius, KayKit, Poly Haven, ambientCG or other CC0 sources, but a single line in the game's credits screen is good practice, for example: "3D models and sound by Kenney (kenney.nl), Quaternius (quaternius.com) and KayKit (kaylousberg.com), CC0."

## Not covered by a credit line

Mixamo (Adobe terms; no redistribution of raw files), Quaternius Bestiary (Quaternius licence), and anything marked **unverified** in the category files need their terms read before use and are deliberately not pre-filled here.
