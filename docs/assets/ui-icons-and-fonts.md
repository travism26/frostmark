# Frostmark: free UI, icon and font assets

Research only. No asset files were downloaded. Every licence below was read from the source's own page (or, for Google Fonts, its `METADATA.pb` and `OFL.txt` in github.com/google/fonts). Where a licence could not be verified it is marked `unverified`. All checks were made on 2026-10-16.

## Best picks for Frostmark

Pick for panels and frames: **Kenney Fantasy UI Borders** (CC0, 140 files, PNG). No attribution, no restrictions, and it is the closest thing in the list to a fantasy inventory frame set. Pair it with **Kenney UI Pack RPG Expansion** for extra buttons and panels.

Pick for skill and item icons: **game-icons.net** (CC BY 3.0). It has several thousand icons, and the flat monochrome style tints easily to a frozen palette. The cost is per-author credit, which must be shipped in the game credits. If you want to avoid credit lists, use **Skymon Icon Pack Free** (CC0, 200+ white 512 px icons) or **Lucifer RPG UI** (CC0, 100+ skill icons plus HUD) instead.

Pick for fonts: **Cinzel** for headings and **Inter** for body and numbers (both SIL OFL 1.1). Cinzel's Roman-capitals look suits a northern outpost, and Inter is readable at inventory sizes.

Style caveat: most free icon and UI packs are pixel art or 2D flat. That clashes with Frostmark's low-poly flat-shaded look unless you tint, recolour or crop them. The Fit column flags this.

## Gaps and caveats

- **Google Fonts pages are JavaScript-rendered.** Licence and designer data was taken from the google/fonts GitHub repo (`METADATA.pb` and `OFL.txt`). Rows marked "Not checked" in the Formats/Count columns still need their `ofl/` folder read.
- **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com/) has no UI or icon packs; its listed packs are 3D only. Its licence (Quaternius Asset License v1.0) is verified: no attribution, and no standalone redistribution. Not useful for this category.
- **Layerlab 2D Inventory Icon** (https://layerlab.itch.io/2d-inventory-icon) is paid ($4.99 or more) and bans use for generative AI training and input. Excluded for both reasons.
- **Summer Engine re-hosted Kenney packs** (cursor, UI, adventure, sci-fi) are re-hosts of Kenney CC0 work. Their pages did not show a licence in this fetch, so use the kenney.nl originals.
- **Godot Asset Library:** searches found mostly CC BY 4.0 themes, so credit lines are needed. No CC0 Godot-native RPG inventory theme was found. The Godot 4.3 to 4.7 support range from the heyheythere listing should be rechecked at adoption time.
- **OpenGameArt pages** rely on the uploader's licence field. Several (496 icons, consolidated, RPG Inventory) mix licences or have disputed histories. Check each item before shipping.
- **Not found / not checked:** Ravenmore and other CC0 fantasy icon sets were not searched in depth. The OpenGameArt "RPG GUI Elements" page returned HTTP 403 and was not checked. A few Kenney URLs returned 404 (Onscreen Controls, Game Icons Expansion Pack) and are excluded.
- **Style fit:** Frostmark uses low-poly flat-shaded art. Most free UI and icon packs are pixel art, so expect to tint, scale or redraw. Flat monochrome icons (game-icons.net, Skymon) are the easiest to fit.
- **Attribution load:** game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) needs per-author credits, which is the main licence cost in the top picks. Keep a credits file in the repo from day one.
