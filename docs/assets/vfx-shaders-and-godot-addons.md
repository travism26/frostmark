# Frostmark: VFX, Shaders and Godot Addons (free options)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Every licence below was read from the source's own page or repo LICENSE on that date; anything I could not read is marked "unverified". Nothing was downloaded or run. "Compat/Web" means the Godot 4.5 GL Compatibility renderer and the HTML5 web export.

## Best picks for Frostmark

For the frozen low-poly look, start with Kenney's Particle Pack (CC0, no strings), `haowg/GODOT-VFX-LIBRARY` (MIT, 35+ GPU-friendly particle scenes and 17+ shaders including frozen, burning, snow and steam; check it runs on Compatibility), and two CC0 godotshaders pieces: the Spatial Ice Shader and Low poly water. For surfaces, the Frost Overlay for Any Mesh shader (MIT) is explicitly tested on Compatibility, and the Sobel outline for Compatibility (CC0) covers outlines. For weather, `Weather FX Free` (CC BY 4.0, credit required) and Sky3D (MIT, supports Compatibility) are web-safe and GDScript-only. For game systems, prefer pure-GDScript MIT addons: Godot State Charts (tick/AI/state), GUT or gdUnit4 (tests), GLoot or Pandora (inventory/items), Dialogue Manager or Dialogic (NPC talk), Phantom Camera (top-down camera). Avoid relying on GDExtension addons (Terrain3D, LimboAI's GDExtension build) for the web build.

**Licence rules at a glance:** No NC, ND or GPL entries were found among the items below. godotshaders.com lets each author choose CC0, MIT or GNU GPL v3 per shader (its /license/ page), so check every shader page; the GPL option exists and a GPL shader would be copyleft. All shaders listed here state CC0 or MIT. MIT requires keeping the copyright and licence notice in copies or substantial portions. CC BY 4.0 requires a credit.

## Gaps and caveats

- No ready-made low-poly 3D hit-impact or magic particle pack with a clean free licence turned up; the free items are textures (Kenney, Brackeys) or a 2D-leaning library (haowg). Paid itch.io packs such as EffectBlocks and Binbun 3D were seen in search but are not free and were not verified.
- I did not read the full text of several repo LICENSE files (Sky3D, ProtonScatter, Maaack, Weather System); those are marked. Godot version ranges for GLoot, Pandora, Dialogic extras, SaveMadeEasy and Input Helper were not checked.
- Web export and Compatibility behaviour is mostly unverified: sources rarely say. Test each shader and particle scene in a real web build. Screen-space/depth effects and 3D-noise sampling are the likeliest problems.
- GDExtension and C++ module addons (Terrain3D, LimboAI) are the main web risk; prefer GDScript addons.
- godotshaders.com mixes CC0, MIT and GPL v3 per shader; its licence covers code only, not the images or textures shown. Re-check each shader page before shipping.
- Search-result summaries were used only to find candidates; every licence in the tables came from a fetched page, except where flagged.
- The Weather FX Free and VFX 2D Free listings state AI assistance in their making. Both need a visible credit under CC BY 4.0.
- Not covered: Kenney smoke sprites on OpenGameArt (licence seen only in search), Unity-asset ports, and itch.io items that were not fetched.
